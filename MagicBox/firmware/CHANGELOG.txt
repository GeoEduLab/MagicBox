FIRMWARE CHANGELOG
==================

Firmware for the ESP32 box: serial_wifi_v2_5_0, which drives all four
sensors, the optional soil, gas and particle sensors, and talks to the application over both USB and WiFi. Newest version
first.

Until 25 September 2026 there was also a USB-only variant, serial_only
(1.x, last v1.3.4), whose versions advanced in step with the WiFi firmware;
the older entries below still say when a change applied to it. It is no
longer shipped.

v2.5.0 — 7 November 2026
------------------------

The test firmware for the newer sensors (2.4.x) becomes the one
MagicBox firmware. Written on BOX_CC9B, BOX_8884 and BOX_2CD1 on 7 November
2026 (every sensor plugged in was found, sensor mounting kept).

Added:

  - The box also reads the newer sensors, when they are plugged in:
    the soil probe (moisture and temperature), the SGP30 and SGP41 gas
    sensors (VOC, and the NOx index for the SGP41) and the BMV080 particle
    sensor (PM1, PM2.5, PM10). Each is optional: a box without them works
    and sends exactly what it sent with 2.3.0, so there is no need to
    reflash the boxes already in schools.
  - Everything listed under 2.4.0 to 2.4.4 below, which until now was only
    a test version: the particle sensor also works with SDA and SCL
    crossed, and the box says when its beam is blocked.

Note:

  - With the particle sensor and the motion sensor in the same box, reading
    the particle sensor takes 0.1-0.2 s and leaves small gaps in the motion
    record. Use the particle sensor in a box of its own, or without the
    motion activities.

v2.4.4 — 22 October 2026 (test version, not in the package)
-----------------------------------------------------------

In serial_wifi_v2_4_4. Flashed on
BOX_D8CD (particle sensor only) on 22 October 2026; the PM values come
as before. Not yet tried with the beam blocked.

Added:

  - The box says when the particle sensor's beam is blocked. A lid, a
    hand or a wall less than about 35 cm above the lens stops the
    measurement. Until now the PM values simply disappeared, and the
    application could not tell a blocked sensor from a missing one. The box
    now sends PM_OBS: 1 instead, and MagicBox 5.8.0 shows a warning.

v2.4.3 — 21 October 2026 (test version, not in the package)
-----------------------------------------------------------

In serial_wifi_v2_4_3. Tried on two
boxes on 21-22 October 2026: BOX_A012 (particle sensor wired with SDA and
SCL crossed) and BOX_D8CD (particle sensor, then the SGP41 on 1.8 V).

Added:

  - The particle sensor works even with its SDA and SCL wires crossed.
    When the BMV080 does not answer, the box looks for it with the two lines
    swapped. If it finds it there it says so in the Serial Monitor
    ([BMV080] found at 0x57 only with SDA and SCL swapped) and reads it
    anyway; the other sensors are not affected. Crossing the two wires back
    is still the better fix.

v2.4.2 — 20 October 2026 (test version, not in the package)
-----------------------------------------------------------

Added:

  - The box can read an SGP41 gas sensor. It sends VOC_IDX and
    NOX_IDX, Sensirion's indices from 1 to 500: for VOC, 100 is the
    room's usual air and higher means more vapours (paint, markers,
    perfume); for NOx, 1 is clean air. The sensor checks itself at start-up
    and needs about an hour to learn the room. Tried on 22 October 2026 on a
    1.8 V board through a level shifter: it works, but only with the board's
    small decoupling capacitor in place.

v2.4.1 — 17 October 2026 (test version, not in the package)
-----------------------------------------------------------

Now in serial_wifi_v2_4_1 (replaces
the 2.4.0 folder). Not yet tried on a box.

Added:

  - **The box can read a BMV080 particulate sensor (DFRobot SEN0663
    board).** It sends PM1, PM25 and PM10: the mass of fine dust and
    smoke in the air, in µg/m³, for particles up to 1, 2.5 and 10 µm
    (the WHO 24-hour guideline for PM2.5 is 15 µg/m³). The readings start
    15 seconds after power-up. The sensor has no fan: it looks with a
    laser at the air about 5 mm above its lens, so nothing may cover it.
    If something stays in front of it (a lid, a wall, a book within about
    35 cm), the sensor notices and the box leaves the PM readings out
    rather than send wrong ones; the Serial Monitor says so. A passing
    hand does no harm.
  - The sensor works only in rooms above 15 °C.
  - Installing it needs one extra library, DFRobot_BMV080, added as a ZIP
    in the Arduino IDE (see the folder's README).

v2.4.0 — 13 October 2026 (test version, not in the package)
-----------------------------------------------------------

A separate test firmware for the newer sensors, kept in
serial_wifi_v2_4_0. The boxes in
schools and the package stay on 2.3.0. The application does not show the
new readings yet.

Added:

  - The box can read an Adafruit STEMMA soil moisture sensor. Plug it
    into a free socket and the box finds it at start-up (it says
    [SOIL] OK at 0x36 in the Serial Monitor) and sends two readings every
    second: SOIL, how wet the soil is, as a raw number (about 200 in dry
    air, up to about 2000 in wet soil; each sensor has to be checked once in
    dry and in wet soil before readings from two sensors can be compared),
    and SOIL_T, the temperature of the sensor's chip (±2 °C; it follows
    the soil only slowly and is not a soil thermometer).
  - The box can read an SGP30 air-quality sensor (GY-SGP30 board). It
    sends TVOC, the volatile organic compounds in the air (ppb: paint,
    markers, perfume, breath), and ECO2, a CO₂ estimate the sensor
    works out from the same gases. ECO2 is not a CO₂ measurement: where
    the box also has the SCD41, trust the SCD41 for CO₂. Both readings
    start 15 seconds after power-up, while the sensor warms up. The box
    corrects them for air humidity with its BME280.
  - The SGP30 needs about 12 hours of running to learn its reference
    level; the box saves it and reuses it after power-off. For a new
    sensor, or a box left unpowered for more than a week, type
    SGP_BASELINE CLEAR in the Serial Monitor and restart the box.
  - A box without the soil sensor or the SGP30 works exactly as on 2.3.0.
    If one of them is pulled out while the box runs, the box stops asking
    it after 10 seconds; plug it back and restart the box.

v2.3.0 — 26 September 2026
--------------------------

Added:

  - The box remembers which way up its motion sensor is mounted. Every
    box built so far has the sensor upside down, and the firmware turns its
    readings over (lying flat it reads +1 g on acceleration Z). That stays the
    default, so a box moving from 2.2.6 to 2.3.0 reads exactly as before.
    A box built on the new board, with the sensor the right way up, is set
    once to "normal": tick "Motion sensor upside down" for it in Settings →
    Box names (the application sends the setting to the box), or type
    IMU_MOUNT NORMAL in the Serial Monitor of the Arduino IDE right after
    flashing. The box keeps it through power-off, reflashing and the WiFi
    reset with the BOOT button, and reads the right way up on every computer.
  - The box announces the setting with its start-up lines
    (IMU_MOUNT: FLIPPED or IMU_MOUNT: NORMAL), and prints those lines
    again when it receives INFO, so the application learns the firmware
    version of a box it joined while the box was already running.
  - Commands work over the USB cable and over WiFi, in capitals or not, with
    any line-ending setting of the Serial Monitor.

Note:

  - Boxes on 2.2.6 do not need this update, and the application does not
    ask for it. Two boxes already in schools stay on 2.2.6 and are correct as
    they are.

Retired: serial_only (USB-only), 25 September 2026
--------------------------------------------------

  - The USB-only firmware serial_only is no longer shipped. The WiFi
    firmware works over the USB cable as well, so one firmware now covers
    every box, whether the class uses the cable, WiFi or both. There is one
    sketch to flash and one guide to follow.
  - **A box still running serial_only should be reflashed with
    serial_wifi_v2_2_6** (now serial_wifi_v2_3_0) (see FLASHING_GUIDE.pdf). Nothing else changes for
    it: over the cable it works exactly as before. The application recognises
    such a box and says so when it connects.

v2.2.6 — 24 September 2026
--------------------------

WiFi boxes only; serial_only stays at v1.3.4, since it has no WiFi.

Changed:

  - The box's own setup network is now called MagicBox-XXXX (it was
    CutiaMagica-XXXX), and its setup page is in English and titled MagicBox,
    like the application. XXXX are still the last four characters of the box ID.
  - The setup page lists the networks the box can actually see, strongest
    first, with signal dots and a padlock for networks with a password; the
    network already saved is preselected. It also says plainly that the box
    sees 2.4 GHz networks only: a router with two bands has to keep its
    2.4 GHz band switched on. A "Show password" tick helps when typing on a
    phone.
  - **A box switched on before the router joins by itself once the router is
    up.** While nobody is connected to its setup page, the box tries the saved
    network again every 2 minutes. Before, it stayed on the setup page until it
    was switched off and on.
  - When a box cannot join, the serial line now says why: network not found
    (out of range, or a 5 GHz-only network), wrong password, or no answer,
    followed by the list of networks it can see. Useful with the Serial
    Monitor of the Arduino IDE when a box refuses the classroom WiFi.
  - The setup page shows a box, not a microscope, next to the box's name, and
    announces itself to phones that support it (DHCP option 114). Some phones,
    Samsung among them, still only say "Internet may not be available": tap
    "Connect only this time" and open 192.168.4.1 in the browser.

Fixed:

  - A network whose name contains an apostrophe or  broke the setup page
    (the name could not be picked from the list). Names are now shown and sent
    exactly as they are.
  - Serial messages no longer contain long dashes, which showed as "???" in
    some terminals.

v2.2.5 (and serial_only v1.3.4) — 23 September 2026
---------------------------------------------------

Changed:

  - Outdoors in sunshine the light reading no longer stops at about 3 800 lux.
    The light sensor used to run at one fixed sensitivity, so
    anything brighter than a well-lit room read the same "3 775". It now picks
    its sensitivity by itself, like a camera, and reads from a dark cupboard
    (below 0.01 lux) up to direct summer sunlight (readings stop at
    150 000 lux). The Light Is Everywhere, Solar Energy and Solar Panels
    activities can now be done outside. Readings under 10 lux carry two
    decimals. Comparisons (shade against sun, morning against noon) are
    reliable at every level; the absolute value above about 10 000 lux has
    not yet been checked against a reference lux meter, so treat it as
    approximate until that bench test is done.
  - The WHITE and RAW light columns keep the scale they always had, so
    files recorded with older firmware can still be compared with new ones;
    in bright light they may now exceed 65 535.
  - Fans, motors and a tapping pencil no longer show up as a fake slow wobble
    in the motion charts. The motion sensor measured vibrations far
    faster than the 50 readings a second the box sends, and those fast shakes
    folded back into slow, invented swings (the same effect that makes wheels
    appear to turn backwards on film). A filter inside the sensor now removes
    everything faster than about 20 per second before it is sampled. Real
    movements of the box — tilting, rocking, shaking it by hand as in
    Resonance — are unaffected. A sharp tap on the table is now caught every
    time instead of only when it happened to fall on a sample, with a
    slightly lower and wider peak than before.
  - The USB-only firmware (serial_only v1.3.4) now reads the CO₂ sensor,
    with the same values and the same 5-second rhythm as the WiFi firmware.
    With v1.3.3 or older, a box connected by USB shows no CO₂ at all.

Fixed:

  - Motion charts no longer freeze for 1.5 s at a time when a firewall blocks the box.
    The box used to wait for an answer
    every five seconds and sent no motion data while it waited. It now keeps
    measuring while it tries, and waits longer between attempts (5 s, 10 s,
    20 s, 40 s, then once a minute). After the firewall is fixed, the box
    connects within a minute, or at once if the laptop application is
    restarted.

Which firmware the activities need:

  - Anything with CO₂ (The Air We Breathe, Indoor Pollution, CO₂ and
    Photosynthesis, Air Quality, Greenhouse Effect): the CO₂ sensor, and
    serial_wifi v2.2.0 or newer, or serial_only v1.3.4 or newer.
  - Light Is Everywhere, Solar Energy, Solar Panels outdoors in sunlight:
    v2.2.5 / v1.3.4 or newer (older firmware stops at about 3 800 lux).
  - Everything else: any version from v2.2.1 / v1.3.0 on (older ones report
    the motion axes upside down).

v2.2.4 — 13 June 2026
---------------------

Fixed:

  - The box could stop sending data until it was unplugged. If a sensor was
    interrupted mid-transfer, it could hold the I²C data line low forever and
    freeze the whole bus. The firmware now recovers the bus by clocking it nine
    times and issuing a stop condition, the same trick used in industrial
    controllers.
  - Sensors are retried during start-up rather than being declared missing on the
    first failure, which cures most "sensor not found" reports caused by a slow
    power-up.
  - Recovery attempts are capped at 30, so a genuinely broken sensor no longer
    puts the box into an endless retry loop that would block the others.

v2.2.3 — 12 June 2026
---------------------

Fixed:

  - The motion sensor (MPU-6500) now heals itself: if it stops responding during
    a lesson, the firmware reinitialises it instead of streaming zeros for the
    rest of the session.

v2.2.2 — 12 June 2026
---------------------

Fixed:

  - The motion sensor disappeared when the CO₂ sensor was fitted. The I²C bus
    ran at 400 kHz, which the SCD41 could not sustain; it corrupted traffic and
    the MPU-6500 vanished from the bus. The bus now runs at 100 kHz, which all
    four sensors tolerate.

v2.2.1 — 12 June 2026
---------------------

Changed:

  - Temperature, humidity and pressure are reported to two decimal places.

Fixed:

  - Corrected the motion sensor's orientation so the reported axes match the way
    the sensor is physically mounted in the box.

v2.2.0 — 12 June 2026
---------------------

Added:

  - CO₂ support (SCD41). The sensor completes a measurement every five
    seconds, which is a hardware limit; the firmware holds the last value and
    repeats it in the 1 Hz packet so the stream stays regular. Adds CO2,
    TEMP_SCD and HUM_SCD to the sensor list.

v2.1.0 — 11 June 2026
---------------------

Added:

  - WiFi setup without editing the sketch. On first start, or whenever no
    saved network is reachable, the box opens an open access point called
    CutiaMagica-XXXX. Connecting to it opens a configuration page
    automatically (captive portal at 192.168.4.1) where the class network and
    password are entered once and stored on the box.

Removed:

  - Hard-coded WiFi credentials. Each box no longer needs its own build.

v2.0.0 — 11 June 2026
---------------------

Added:

  - WiFi transport. The laptop application broadcasts a UDP beacon every
    three seconds on port 8766; the box listens for it, opens a TCP connection
    and streams the same lines it prints over USB. The application discards
    duplicates by timestamp, so running both transports at once is safe and a
    cable can be unplugged mid-lesson without losing the session.

The versions below predate WiFi support. They are the same sensor code this
firmware grew out of, kept here because the faults they fixed still explain
how the box behaves.

v1.2.0 — 11 June 2026
---------------------

Added:

  - The firmware version is printed at boot (FW: x.y.z), so the version in use
    can be confirmed without opening the sketch.

Fixed:

  - The motion-sensor mounting correction is now a proper 180° rotation rather
    than a mirrored Z axis, which had left one axis with the wrong sign.

v1.1.0 — 2 May 2026
-------------------

Fixed:

  - Every box reported the same identifier. The device ID was derived from
    the part of the MAC address shared by all ESP32 chips; it now uses the unique
    bytes, so BOX_XXXX differs per box — which is what makes multi-box
    activities such as Air Quality possible.
  - The 1 Hz data packet is always sent, even when one sensor fails, so a single
    faulty sensor no longer silences the whole box.
  - Gyroscope values gained a third decimal place, and the output buffer was
    enlarged to prevent truncated lines on long packets.

v1.0.0 — 2 May 2026
-------------------

First firmware: VEML7700 light, BME280 temperature/humidity/pressure and
MPU-6500 motion over USB serial at 115200 baud, emitting one JSON line per
second plus a 50 Hz motion stream.
